# Dual Disease Burden: Primary Immunodeficiency and Non-Cystic Fibrosis Bronchiectasis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project investigates the clinical outcomes of patients with primary immunodeficiency (PID) and non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis (NCFB) through a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Primary immunodeficiency can predispose patients to recurrent respiratory infections, chronic airway inflammation, and progressive lung damage. When bronchiectasis develops in patients with primary immunodeficiency, the combination may result in a substantial clinical burden.

The aim of this systematic review is to synthesise the available evidence on clinical outcomes in patients with both conditions.

The primary outcome is annual mortality, while secondary outcomes include:

- Annual all-cause hospitalisation
- Annual exacerbation rate
- FEV1 decline
- Quality of life

Where available, outcomes are also compared between patients with NCFB with and without primary immunodeficiency.

## 🗂️ Project Structure

The project is divided into the following main steps:

- Define the research question and PEO framework
- Develop the search strategy
- Identify relevant studies
- Remove duplicate records
- Screen titles and abstracts
- Perform full-text screening
- Extract study and outcome data
- Assess study quality / risk of bias
- Prepare data for analysis
- Perform statistical analysis
- Conduct meta-analysis
- Interpret and report findings

### 🔎 Define the Research Question

_In patients with non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis, what are the clinical outcomes associated with co-existing primary immunodeficiency, particularly annual mortality, hospitalisation, exacerbations, FEV1 decline, and quality of life?_

| PEO Element | Definition |
|-------------|------------|
| **Population** | Patients with non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis |
| **Exposure** | Primary immunodeficiency / inborn errors of immunity |
| **Outcomes** | Mortality, hospitalisation, exacerbations, FEV1 decline and quality of life |
